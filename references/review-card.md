# Candidate Review Card

Score each candidate from 1-5. A candidate should not move forward if product fidelity, physical plausibility, or the visual soul scores below 3.

| Dimension | What to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Visual soul | Does it carry the board's rhythm, light, texture, and camera behavior without copying a frame? |
| Life and action | Does the action look like an unplanned moment rather than a demonstration? |
| Product fidelity | Are silhouette, material, seams, controls, hardware, closures, and scale recognizable? |
| Physics | Are anatomy, hand contact, weight, straps, shadows, reflections, and perspective believable? |
| Camera truth | Does the focal length, crop, depth, motion, and foreground relationship make photographic sense? |
| Light and color | Is the light source coherent, the exposure livable, and the palette appropriate to the soul board? |
| Editorial taste | Does the result avoid a stiff catalog or glossy CGI feel? |

## Revision routing

| Failing layer | Change next time |
| --- | --- |
| Too staged | Simplify the action, remove eye contact, add an in-between moment or foreground interruption. |
| Too dark or cinematic | Specify open available light, the source direction, reflected fill, and brighter exposure tolerance. |
| Product drifts | Repeat fewer but more concrete invariants; use more product views if available. |
| Weak life | Change the situation, not just the camera angle. Add a plausible reason for the person to be there. |
| Bad anatomy or contact | Reduce the action to one hand and one object; state the support or weight relationship. |
| Wrong lens feel | State camera distance and perspective behavior, not only the focal-length number. |

Keep a short shot log: candidate filename, product, soul card, lens, action, light, score, and one failing layer. Successful candidates become future positive references; failed candidates become narrow negative examples.
