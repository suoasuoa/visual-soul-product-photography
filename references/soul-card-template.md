# Visual Soul Card Template

Use one card per brand or collection. It should describe transferable relationships, not copyrighted campaigns, creators, brand names, or exact reference layouts.

```text
NAME
<short internal name>

AUDIENCE / PRODUCT ROLE
<who uses the product and what kind of life it participates in>

HUMAN RHYTHM
<2-4 ordinary actions: walking, pausing, talking, carrying, making, resting>

PLACE AND MATERIAL
<plausible settings, surfaces, local objects, weather, background density>

LIGHT
<time of day, direction, softness, contrast, reflected fill, exposure tolerance>

CAMERA BEHAVIOR
<35mm / 50-58mm / 85mm roles, camera height, crop, foreground obstruction, movement>

COLOR AND TEXTURE
<palette, grain, fabric/skin/material cues, saturation restraint>

TASTE FLOOR
<the emotional quality that must be present>

PRODUCT INTEGRATION
<how the product should participate in life, not be demonstrated>

AVOID
<only the recurring failure modes: hard-ad posing, low-key gloom, CGI, impossible anatomy, etc.>
```

## Example of the level of specificity

```text
HUMAN RHYTHM
People caught mid-route, an easy conversation, a short pause at a local shop.

LIGHT
Bright available daylight or warm side light; shadows retain detail; slight exposure variation is welcome.

CAMERA BEHAVIOR
35mm for place and movement, 50-58mm for ordinary use, 85mm for a nearby observation through foreground texture.

TASTE FLOOR
Lived-in, unforced, tactile, and a little imperfect rather than polished or dramatic.
```
